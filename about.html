About me

I'm Noholoa*, an artist and maker working at the intersection of science and art. This web site is a home for my side projects: sci-art that merges scientific thinking with visual expression, original music and poetry, constructed languages, and other things I build along the way.

My interests span the visual, the sonic, the linguistic, and the scientific. I really believe in exploring ideas deeply and expressing them through whatever medium attracts my attention.

Previous work includes art on the Moon, an archive of private, celestially themed artwork I curated from around the world that was carried to the lunar surface in 2024 aboard Intuitive Machines' Odysseus lander, and MWL, a pictographic language for adventure fiction relying on shared astronomical phenomena.

Each pursuit tends to feed the others, often creating unexpected connections.

* Noholoa is the online name of Alex Noholoa Windell.
